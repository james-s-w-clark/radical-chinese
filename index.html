Common Radicals Guide 常用部首

Most Chinese characters (over 80%) are Phono-Semantic Compounds (形声字). They combine a Semantic Radical (形旁) that gives the meaning category, with a Phonetic Component (声旁) that hints at the sound!

氵
shuǐ (三点水)
Water / Liquid / Oceans
Examples: 海 (ocean), 江 (river), 洗 (wash), 洋 (vast sea)

Female / Woman / Kinship
Examples: 妈 (mother), 姐 (sister), 妹 (younger sister), 好 (good)
